How to play: Use your arrow keys to move the tiles. When two shoes touch, they get better!

A blue ballet flat opens the shelf at 2, and a rainbow platform mule closes it at 2048. The pairs in between: a green ankle-strap sandal, a fuchsia embroidered stiletto, a yellow platform pump, a leopard-print pump, a black patent sandal, a polka-dot pump, a blue strappy sandal, a green glitter lace-up and a houndstooth platform heel. Slide the 4×4 shelf with the arrow keys or a swipe; two identical pairs become the next style and add points. Leave a few squares empty and plan ahead, because a full board with no matching pair ends the spree. The rainbow mule is the pair to reach.
